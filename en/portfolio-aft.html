About this Project

Afusion is a web design and technology services team spanning Taiwan, Canada, the Philippines, and Singapore. This redesign covers three core sections: the Join AFT careers page, the About company page, and the Contact page. The design uses candid team photos as the primary visual, layered with hand-drawn doodle-style illustrations, bringing a lively, authentic office culture to the professional tech feel — echoing the work style of an American startup.

#14CDD9 - AFT Blue
#131522 - AFT Black
Join AFT — Careers Page
A 3×3 grid highlights company culture (office environment, team events, benefits, and more), each tile paired with candid staff photos and hand-drawn doodle icons — replacing dry bullet lists with a light, playful tone so candidates get a feel for the real work environment.
About — Company Profile
An interactive map shows the four locations — Canada, the Philippines, Singapore, and Taiwan. Clicking a city switches to candid photos from that office, highlighting the scale of the team's cross-border collaboration and cultural diversity.
Contact — Contact Page
Combines the office location map and the contact form on a single page — visitors can first confirm their nearest office on the map, then submit an inquiry directly, shortening the path from contact to response.
