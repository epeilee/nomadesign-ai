About Logo

This collection spans brand logo design across different industries — each project starts from the meaning behind its name and develops into an identity system that extends to app icons, business cards, and other collateral.

JH is a tech/studio brand; its hexagonal lettermark conveys a rational, structured tech tone.

T.work is a coworking-space brand; its calligraphic wordmark evokes a sense of humanity paired with flexible space.

eParking is a parking app brand; its clean icon mark emphasizes fast recognition and intuitive operation.

iSky Travel is a travel brand; its paper-airplane motion mark conveys a sense of exploration and movement.
