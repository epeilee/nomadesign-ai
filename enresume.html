About Me

I'm Rachel Lee, a designer passionate about UI, web, and visual design. I graduated from Ming Chuan University with a Bachelor's degree in Commercial Design. My friendly and outgoing nature helps me enrich my life experiences. In my free time, I love to travel, read novels, enjoy fitness, and watch dramas.

As a UI designer, I have successfully executed comprehensive design processes, including Design Systems, Flowcharts, Wireframes, UI flows, Prototypes, and Mockups, delivering these to engineers for development. Before that, I was an in-house designer responsible for product interface design as well as the company's corporate identity and promotional materials. During my time at an advertising agency, I designed corporate identity systems and external promotional materials for clients, accumulating valuable experience in graphic and packaging design, along with a strong understanding of corporate branding guidelines and advertising print processes.

I changed career from nurse to designer, starting with graphic design, then web design, and finally diving deep into UI/UX design. My passion for design drives me to continuously explore new trends and create innovative styles. My design style leans toward simplicity and elegance, emphasizing a balance between visual appeal and functionality. I am good at working independently and can also collaborate efficiently with a team.

I am ready to embrace new innovations and challenges. I look forward to integrating my design skills into my new job. Thank you for your time, and I hope to grow together with your company!
